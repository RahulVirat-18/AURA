Security & Privacy

How your data is protected

Data Processing

System metrics are processed locally on your machine. Only aggregated summary data is transmitted to the dashboard for visualization. Your raw system information remains on your device.

Data Protection

All communication is encrypted using industry-standard security protocols. User credentials are securely hashed and stored. We do not store unnecessary data and maintain minimal logging for security purposes.

Your Control

You have full control over your data. Adjust notification preferences, data retention, and monitoring intervals anytime through the Settings panel. You can pause or stop monitoring at any time.

Transparency

We are committed to protecting your privacy. AURA is designed with privacy-first principles - local processing, minimal data transmission, and no third-party sharing of your system information.
